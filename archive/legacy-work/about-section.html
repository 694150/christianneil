THE PERSON & THE BACKGROUND

More about me.

Graphic Artist & Website Developer.

DESIGN / DEVELOP / PRODUCE

Hi, I’m Neil.

I’m a Graphic Artist and Website Developer based in Cavite, Philippines, with over nine years of experience across website development, graphic design, and print production. I help turn ideas into visuals and websites that feel thoughtful, communicate clearly, and have personality.

My work has connected me with businesses in the Philippines, Australia, and the United States. From creating brand identities and building WordPress websites to preparing artwork for print, I’ve learned to consider both how a design looks and how it works in its final form.

I enjoy the process of bringing an idea together—exploring directions, working through feedback, and refining the small details. My background in development and production helps me carry that care through to the finished work.

Outside work, you’ll usually find me gaming or playing pickleball—a little competition and a chance to recharge.
